# Hero artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool, using the selected Story World mockup as a composition and style reference. Output original is retained in `work/story-world-original.png` (local working file, excluded from Git). The production files are `public/assets/story-world.webp` and `public/assets/story-world-small.webp`.

Prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: standalone illustrated background artwork for a responsive portfolio website hero, landscape 3:2. Use only the scene at the top of the approved Story World mockup as the composition and style reference; do not reproduce the website or anything below the hero. A polished warm 2D animated-film suburban American neighborhood. Left foreground: welcoming cream dental clinic with burnt orange awning, friendly brown-haired female dentist in white coat and teal scrubs talking with a brown-haired female patient in dusty rose blouse. Right foreground: friendly Black HVAC technician with dark gray polo and tablet talking with brown-haired male homeowner in navy polo and khaki trousers, white HVAC service van and cream suburban home behind. Tree foliage naturally frames upper left and right edges, flowers around bottom corners; leafy sunny street recedes down center. Softly shaded hand-drawn cartoon aesthetic like the reference, warm golden daylight, blue sky, organic details. Preserve large empty central and upper sky region for live HTML typography. People and buildings occupy lower half and far side edges. Center upper 55 percent must be readable open pale blue sky. Wide full-bleed composition; characters visible from head to waist at lower edge. All four figures in lower outer quarters, no person in central third. Only artwork, no typography, labels, logos, numbers, buttons, video frames or website UI. No navy section at bottom, orange line or film strip. Blank unlettered clinic sign and van panels.


The films are Piyush's existing supplied work. This project did not generate or alter the finished videos. Their extracted posters show actual film frames.
